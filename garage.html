My Dream Garage

This page showcases the three BMWs I would choose to have in my dream garage, with each car serving a different purpose: a practical daily driver, an exciting weekend car, and a special dream collectible. Each of these cars represents something different that I appreciate about BMW, whether it is comfort and everyday usability, powerful performance and an exciting driving experience, or classic styling and automotive history. Together, these three BMWs create my ideal garage by combining modern technology, impressive performance, practicality, and timeless BMW character. Each car has its own personality and represents a different reason why I have always been interested in BMW and its M models.

BMW M5 CS


2021 - Limited Production


The M5 CS is the most powerful M5 ever made. It combines the performance of a supercar with the comfort and practicality of a luxury sedan. With a lightweight design, upgraded chassis, and a high-revving twin-turbo V8, the M5 CS delivers incredible speed while still being usable as a daily driver.

BMW M3 Competition(G80)


2021 - Present


The M3 Competition is a modern high-performance sedan that offers impressive power, sharp handling, and a bold design. It uses a twin-turbo inline-6 engine and an advanced chassis, making it a very capable car on both the street and the track. The M3 Competition continues the M3's legacy while introducing a more aggressive and modern design.

BMW M3 (E46)


2001 - 2006


The E46 M3 is considered a modern classic and is one of the most loved M cars. It features a naturally aspirated inline-6 engine, a balanced chassis, and a clean and timeless design. The E46 M3 is known for its analog driving feel and remains a favorite among BMW enthusiasts..
