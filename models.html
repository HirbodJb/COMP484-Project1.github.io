BMW M Models

This page highlights selected BMW M cars and briefly explores their performance and character. From compact and agile to powerful and refined, each M model brings a unique driving experience while staying true to BMW's motorsport heritage.

BMW M2

A compact sports coupe with bold styling and a driver-focused character. The M2 brings the M experience to a smaller two-door package.

View Details
  • Body style: Two-door coupe
  • Engine: 3.0-liter twin-turbocharged inline-six
  • Horsepower: 453 hp
  • Drive Type: RWD

BMW M5 Competition

A performance sedan that combines a spacious four-door layout with a sporty character. The M5 Competition brings luxury and performance together.

View Details
  • Body style: four-door sedan
  • Engine: 4.4-liter twin-turbocharged V8
  • Horsepower: 617 hp
  • Drive Type: xDrive

BMW X6 M

A sporty SUV with a sloping roofline and a bold presence. The X6 M combines a higher driving position with the character of the M range.

View Details
  • Body style: four-door SUV
  • Engine: 4.4-liter twin-turbocharged V8 hybrid
  • Horsepower: 617 hp
  • Drive Type: xDrive

Why BMW M Stands Out

BMW M combines everyday usability with high performance. M cars are designed to be practical enough for daily driving, while also delivering an engaging, emotional driving experience. With motorsport heritage, precision engineering, and distinctive design, BMW M offers a unique blend of performance, technology, and character that sets it apart.
